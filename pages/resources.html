Resources overview

The Resources pages provide access to key information, FAQs, guidance materials, and supporting documents related to GBDI’s work, designed to help stakeholders better understand building data, interoperability, and sustainability across the built environment.

As GBDI’s work continues to evolve, this section will grow to include additional insights that help enable informed decision-making, collaboration, and the implementation of data-driven approaches across the industry.
