The importance of Building Data Reporting

Collecting and reporting building data is essential for informed decision-making. It empowers stakeholders to implement effective carbon reduction strategies.

For Policy Makers
Utilize benchmarks to develop targeted carbon reduction strategies for your region or sector.

For Building Owners
Understand your building’s carbon footprint for compliance, marketing, and enhancing real estate value.

For Designers
Compare your design performance and improvement potentials through benchmarking and hotspot analysis.

Open Data Benefits
Data transparency leads to better collaboration and innovative solutions for sustainable building practices.
