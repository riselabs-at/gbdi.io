Open Building Data Platform

Make net-zero whole life cycle (WLC) emissions buildings the new norm using GBDI’s open building data platform. Identify scalable strategies for embodied carbon reduction and removal, and improve building life cycle performance.

Get actionable insights and learn from best-in-class examples and emerging practice to set tangible targets and create effective policy.
