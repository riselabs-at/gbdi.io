High quality whole life cycle data is essential for informed decision making and effective development of building portfolios. GBDI empowers stakeholders to implement effective decarbonization strategies and robust climate policy.

Get in touch

Policy Makers
Use the open building data format (openBDF) to facilitate exchange  of building life cycle data. The open building data platform offers you open-source data management and advanced analytics to implement monitoring, verification, and reporting (MRV). We help you understand the life cycle climate performance of regional and national building stocks to design and implement effective policies.




Portfolio Managers
Understand the carbon footprint of buildings in your portfolio to check compliance, assess value, and identify potential for cost-effective improvements to ensure robust development of your assets through challenging times. GBDI’s open formats and platform help you facilitate data exchange across partners to reduce friction and advance insights.

Building Designers
Improve your design through detailed comparison against a global database of best-practice cases relevant to your context. Identify hotspots of resource use and emissions using our systematic protocol. We help you identify effective strategies for optimizing the life cycle performance of your buildings.
